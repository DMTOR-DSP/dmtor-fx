FX

Every effect in the lib, 27 of them: what each one does and what you can turn. They come from NOEMA (the Bliss and Noise editions), Speak & Grain and RUPTURE, and they're all plain C++17 that runs on a phone or a Daisy Seed.

  • Send Takes the send and returns only the wet signal; mix it back in.
  • In place Replaces the sound with the processed sound.
  • Chain Several of the other effects in a fixed order.

Space and time 11

Reverbs, delays, resonators and clouds: they add room, repeats and memory.

Space

Send
RUPTURE

The big reverb. Four structures built from ChaosVerb's reverb, a pre-delay of up to 2.5 s with its own feedback so it echoes into the wash, and an element after the tail. Shimmer, Gate, Freeze and Chaos sit on top, and the tail can duck under what you play. It follows a clock, and changing structure crossfades.

Controls

  • Structure Ultra, Cathedral, Prism or Gravity
  • Decay
  • Tone dark to bright
  • Pre-delay 0 to 2.5 s
  • Pre-delay feedback up to 70 %
  • Element the shimmer's interval, or an effect after the tail
  • Shimmer the tail pitched and fed back
  • Gate a tempo gate on the tail
  • Freeze the tail holds forever
  • Chaos rhythmic gating and frozen stabs
  • Duck the tail dips under the dry signal
Structures, types and elements
Structures
  • Ultra a plate (Dattorro's tank): dense, moody, toward endless
  • Cathedral a large 8-line hall with slow, deep modulation
  • Prism two halls, one per side, pitched −12 on the left and +7 on the right
  • Gravity the hall played backwards through a window, so the tail swells toward the next hit
Elements
  • Shimmer +12, +7, −12 the interval the shimmer moves by
  • Chorus two slow voices
  • Vibrato in eighth notes
  • Vowel formant filters through a, e, i, o, u
  • Tremolo in eighth notes
  • Lo-Fi tape wow, then bits and rate lost

Echo

Send
RUPTURE

The big delay. Two lines of up to 2.7 s in six structures, a type that runs from clean digital through BBD to tape, routing from parallel to ping-pong, and an element inside the feedback loop that compounds with every repeat. Feedback goes past 100 %, held back by saturation and a guard. The time glides when changed, bending the pitch like tape, and the whole thing follows a clock.

Controls

  • Time up to 2.7 s
  • Structure six, below
  • Type digital → BBD → tape
  • Routing parallel → series → ping-pong
  • Feedback 0 to 110 %
  • Element six, below, inside the loop
  • Depth the element's amount and the depth of a one-bar LFO on the time
  • Chaos on a beat the time jumps (half, double, dotted) and glides back
  • Freeze the loop repeats forever, no new input
Structures, types and elements
Structures
  • Standard two lines side by side
  • Multitap four taps
  • Multi Filter the four taps through their own band-pass filters, panned apart
  • Poly the second line at 3:2, 4:3 or 5:4 of the first
  • Reverse every repeat backwards
  • Series one line feeds the other
Types
  • Digital clean
  • BBD dark and companded, with clock noise
  • Tape wow, flutter, darkening, saturation, hiss and dropouts
Elements
  • Pitch each repeat down an octave, up a fifth or up an octave
  • Granulize the repeats break into grains
  • Crush each repeat loses bits and rate
  • Filter a band-pass swept by the LFO, for dub throws
  • Diffuse the repeats blur toward a reverb
  • Cassette saturation, a darker top and more wow

ChaosVerb Reverb

Send
RUPTUREfrom the ChaosVerb pedal

The reverb Space is built from, usable on its own: a bright, dense plate, an 8-line hall with slow, deep modulation, or the hall with a shimmer in its feedback. In front of it are a pre-delay synced to the beat, with tape wobble, and a 150 Hz low cut. Switching between plate and hall crossfades.

Controls

  • Size a short room to near-infinite
  • Tone dark to bright
  • Type plate, hall or shimmer
  • Chaos a rhythmic gate on the tail and occasional frozen stabs
  • Freeze endless, no new input
  • Duck depth how far the tail dips under the dry signal

Cloud

Send
NOEMA Bliss

A granular cloud. It sprays the last few seconds back as soft grains: delayed, scattered, pitched up, sometimes reversed. Freeze holds its memory. Echo's Granulize element is this cloud.

Controls

  • Density about 1.5 to 60 grains a second
  • Size about 20 ms to 1.6 s
  • Scatter up to about 3.5 s back
  • Pitch how high grains climb
  • Up chance
  • Reverse
  • Spread
  • Sweep
  • Freeze

  • Reverse
