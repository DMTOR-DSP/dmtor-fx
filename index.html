Synths 7

Instruments you play: from analogue-style polys to generative drones.

VOLTENNE

Mac

Expressive polyphonic analogue-style synth.

  • Two band-limited oscillators per voice into the SILK-24 and EDGE-24 four-pole lowpasses and a resonant HP-12
  • Eight voices (up to 16), poly-mod, Voice Age drift, Slip, Push, Filter Slam and Filter Motion

DMTOR-DSP/voltenne · private

VOLTENNE FLUX

Mac, iPad

VOLTENNE with the effects as the instrument: the keys play loops, grains, glitches and tuned echoes.

  • A 32-second memory feeds nine worlds (Cloud, Tape, Cascade, Resonate, Drone, Strum, Arp, Shatter, Incant), alone or layered
  • Tuned to the chord you hold, through the SILK / EDGE filter, four reverbs and a phrase looper

DMTOR-DSP/voltenne-flux · private

CIRRAVOLT

Mac, iPad

Aggressive mono and hybrid lead and bass synth.

  • Two analogue oscillators (sync, FM, PWM) and a wavetable oscillator into three filters: EDGE-24, LADDER-24, VECTOR-12
  • BITE, CONTOUR, FURY, tuned feedback and Bass Lock; a 64-step sequencer with Mutate, Undo and Return
  • Dubstep edition: master wobble, four styles, 16 presets

DMTOR-DSP/cirravolt · private

DUB VOLT

Mac, iPad

A simple dubstep synth built from CIRRAVOLT's engine: twelve sounds and a few knobs.

DMTOR-DSP/dubvolt · private

VEXMORPH

Mac

A controlled-instability instrument: make something alive, push it past its limits, find your way back.

  • A tonal body excites a bank of eight bounded modal resonators, both feeding a nonlinear feedback network
  • One MUTATION macro walks each patch from stable to near collapse; RETURN glides home

DMTOR-DSP/vexmorph · private

NOOEMA

Android, iPhone, iPad

One app, several generative ambient and drone synthesizers: pick an edition, touch the world, and it blooms.

  • Apps in it: NOEMA BLISS, NOEMA NOISE, NOOEMA DOOM, FEEDBACK, RITUAL, TAPE and VOID
  • NOOEMA STUDIO plays several editions at once on a large iPad
  • A generative brain, granular engine, choir, listening mode and an automatic composer

DMTOR-DSP/noema · private

Undertow

Mac, Daisy hardware

Mono, duo, para and poly voice with seven filter types, a fixed filter bank, an arpeggiator and a 64-step sequencer.

  • Also a hardware synth for the Daisy Seed3 Desktop Dev Kit: eight pots, sixteen buttons, two toggles

DMTOR-DSP/daisy · private

Built FX units and combos 7

Finished effect boxes, and effects chained into one instrument.

RUPTURE

Daisy hardware

A stereo end-of-chain effects box: SPACE and DAMAGE in an order you pick, a 60-second looper, and 16 pads that play the effects.

  • SPACE: a four-structure reverb and a dual delay with tape and BBD types
  • DAMAGE: glitch, grains, distortion and noise

ChaosVerb

Hothouse pedal

A reverb and glitch pedal for the Hothouse on a Daisy Seed3: plate, hall and shimmer.

FRACTURE

Mac

Heavy distortion that stays defined: kicks become a solid harmonic tone with their punch kept.

  • Six controls, one job each: character (warm, clip, fuzz, fold, square, grind, diode), drive, punch and more

Drum machines and sequencers 3

Beats, kits, samplers and generators that run the clock.

NOEMA BEATS

Android, iPhone, iPad

The drum machine inside the NOEMA apps: generative beats on its own drum samples, with a yoga kit in BLISS.

  • Beats come from pattern tables that follow the generative brain and the clock
  • In NOEMA BLISS and NOEMA NOISE

DMTOR-DSP/noema · private

SÉANCE DRUMS and SAMPLER

iPad

A drum machine with 1,000+ kits, per-pad body, pitch, snap, filter and drive, and a Digitakt-style sampler.

  • Pads can go to a group bus; sidechain pump meter and key
  • Sampler: draw filter, shape and movement; slice to 16 pads; 32 take slots; loops always play in time with the clock

DMTOR-DSP/? · private

LANES

Mac

A generative MIDI sequencer and master clock: pick a vibe, press play, and it writes drums, bass, chords, pads, lead and arp across three lanes.

BLEAK

Android, Mac

A generative, sound-reactive visual instrument: sound becomes structure, structure becomes damage.

  • Dozens of engines, four layers with an A/B mixer, played by hand on the screen
  • Reads a mic or PA, locks kicks and snares to the beat, records and renders video

SÉANCE

iPad

A mixer for the iPad built from CONDUIT: eight channels of synths or inputs, aux returns, mastering effects, XY mangle pads, MIDI learn and BLEAK one key away.

  • Hardware inserts to ADAT, sidechain on channels and groups, a MIDI channel per strip, one clock that follows MIDI clock
