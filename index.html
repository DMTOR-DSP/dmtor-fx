Every effect in the library 34

The effects and engines in the DMTØR lib, from NOEMA, Speak & Grain, RUPTURE and REMNANT, with what you can turn.

Space

RUPTURE · Send

The big reverb. Four structures built from ChaosVerb's reverb, a pre-delay of up to 2.5 s with its own feedback so it echoes into the wash, and an element after the tail. Shimmer, Gate, Freeze and Chaos sit on top, and the tail can duck under what you play. It follows a clock, and changing structure crossfades.

Controls: Structure, Decay, Tone, Pre-delay, Pre-delay feedback, Element, Shimmer, Gate, Freeze, Chaos, Duck

More

Structures

  • Ultra a plate (Dattorro's tank): dense, moody, toward endless
  • Cathedral a large 8-line hall with slow, deep modulation
  • Prism two halls, one per side, pitched −12 on the left and +7 on the right
  • Gravity the hall played backwards through a window, so the tail swells toward the next hit

Elements

  • Shimmer +12, +7, −12 the interval the shimmer moves by
  • Chorus two slow voices
  • Vibrato in eighth notes
  • Vowel formant filters through a, e, i, o, u
  • Tremolo in eighth notes
  • Lo-Fi tape wow, then bits and rate lost

Echo

RUPTURE · Send

The big delay. Two lines of up to 2.7 s in six structures, a type that runs from clean digital through BBD to tape, routing from parallel to ping-pong, and an element inside the feedback loop that compounds with every repeat. Feedback goes past 100 %, held back by saturation and a guard. The time glides when changed, bending the pitch like tape, and the whole thing follows a clock.

Controls: Time, Structure, Type, Routing, Feedback, Element, Depth, Chaos, Freeze

More

Structures

  • Standard two lines side by side
  • Multitap four taps
  • Multi Filter the four taps through their own band-pass filters, panned apart
  • Poly the second line at 3:2, 4:3 or 5:4 of the first
  • Reverse every repeat backwards
  • Series one line feeds the other

Types

  • Digital clean
  • BBD dark and companded, with clock noise
  • Tape wow, flutter, darkening, saturation, hiss and dropouts

Elements

  • Pitch each repeat down an octave, up a fifth or up an octave
  • Granulize the repeats break into grains
  • Crush each repeat loses bits and rate
  • Filter a band-pass swept by the LFO, for dub throws
  • Diffuse the repeats blur toward a reverb
  • Cassette saturation, a darker top and more wow

Grain Engine

Speak & Grain · Buffer

Speak & Grain's granular engine. It plays a recording, or the last few seconds of live input, as clouds of short, smoothly windowed grains: up to five clouds at once, one per finger, each around its own place in the sound at its own pitch. Grains can play backwards and spread across the stereo field, positions glide, and it never clicks. It reports every grain so a screen can draw it. The whole app, with its glitch, delay, reverb and dry/wet, is the lib's examples/speakgrain/Instrument.

Controls: Grain size, Density, Spray, Reverse, Stereo spread, Position, Pitch, Own size, density, spray, Source

Remnant

REMNANT · Buffer

An ambient micro-looper after the Chase Bliss MOOD. It's always listening, so Capture keeps the last few seconds you already played; Record and Arm take a phrase forward. Loops play at half to double speed, stepped in octaves, fifths and fourths or free, reverse through a glide, stretch (granular: slower without changing pitch, down to a held moment), overdub with limited feedback, freeze with an optional fade, and stutter while held. Seams crossfade from real pre-roll and adapt to how alike the two sides are, so a drone holds its level. Up to 43 s of stereo. Slip, the wet channel's pitch voice, keeps sampling the input, the loop or both and plays it back up to two octaves either way, in semitones or in octaves, fifths and fourths, forward or reversed: a pitch shifter at short windows, phrases that follow behind you at long ones, a melody when frozen. Captures keep what Slip played, so pitch stacks on pitch. A reverb on its own level (up to twice the dry, to cloud it), RUPTURE's Space (cathedral, ultra, prism or gravity, with shimmer and freeze), washes the input, the loop or both, and Slip; captures keep its trails, so a frozen reverb becomes a loop. Last, NOEMA Noise's glitch stutters it all on the beat grid (how often, slice, hold, pitch, mix), kept out of the recordings; a tempo or an external MIDI clock runs the grid, and Sync snaps the loop, Slip and the pre-delay to note values. Its clock reducer, filter and warble come next.

Controls: Capture, Record, Arm, Length, Speed, Reverse, Stretch, Overdub, Feedback, Freeze, Freeze decay, Stutter, Dry, Loop, Input, Seam, Wet, Wet input, Pitch, Pitch steps, Window, Slip reverse, Wet freeze, Reverb, Reverb input, Structure, Decay, Tone, Pre-delay, Shimmer, Reverb freeze, Glitch, Glitch mix, Slice, Hold, Glitch pitch, Tempo, Sync
